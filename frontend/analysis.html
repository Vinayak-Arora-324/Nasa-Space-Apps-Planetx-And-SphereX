How to read this

SPHEREx looks through a linear variable filter: each exposure sees a given spot of sky at a different wavelength, and each of the six detectors covers its own range (0.75–5 µm). So a movie in time order is also a walk through wavelength — a star can brighten or fade between frames simply because the filter moved to a different colour.

To separate real change from spectral shape, compare frames of the same detector: use Epoch stacks or Difference. Objects that move shift position between epochs (about six months apart); the yellow marker shows where a catalogue object should be given its proper motion. Orientation: north is up, east is left.

Data: NASA/IPAC Infrared Science Archive (SPHEREx), Gaia DR3 (ESA), CatWISE2020, CDS (Sesame, Aladin Lite, HiPS surveys).
