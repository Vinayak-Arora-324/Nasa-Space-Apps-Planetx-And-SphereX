Gaia data only inside a 3° cone around the patch. The rest of the sky shows the bright stars from the XHIP catalogue.

The Milky Way glow is NASA SVS Deep Star Maps 2020 (Hipparcos, Tycho-2, Gaia DR2) with the resolved stars removed. It is the sky seen from the Sun, so it fades as you fly far away.

Evidence view · only measured and catalogue values drawn

Fly through the patch

Click empty sky to fly · WASD move · mouse look · wheel throttle · T auto/manual speed · Shift boost · G autopilot to the target · Esc release

  • G flies to the selected star, or the one under the crosshair. Esc cancels the autopilot or releases the mouse.
  • Tab jumps to the Sun's system. E switches the evidence view, which hides everything illustrative. H returns to the start view. [ and ] change the magnitude limit.
  • Auto speed (on by default) makes your speed a fraction of the distance to the nearest star or planet, so you slow down as you arrive and cannot fly into one. The wheel sets that fraction; T switches to a fixed speed. Shift goes ten times faster. R and Space go up, F goes down.
  • Touch: one finger looks around, two fingers pinch to move, a tap names a star.
